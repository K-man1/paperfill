Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Paperfill fills out worksheets for you. That means you hand us documents, and documents are personal. This page is a plain description of what we collect, who else sees it, and how long we keep it.

The short version. Your uploaded worksheets are sent to third-party AI providers so they can be read and filled in. They are deleted from our servers after 30 days. We do not sell your data. Don't upload anything confidential.

Who runs Paperfill

Paperfill is operated by an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at paperfill@user.hackclub.app with any question about this policy or about your data.

What we collect

Account information If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and profile picture. If you sign up with an email address, we store the address, your name, and a cryptographic hash of your password (never the password itself).
Documents you upload The PDFs and images you submit to be filled, and the filled versions we produce. If you use the handwriting feature, the template page you write on and the font generated from it.
Usage records How many fills you have run, which AI models handled them, and the resulting cost. This is how the daily free-credit limit works.
Device information We set a cookie containing a random device identifier that lasts two years, and record your IP address, browser user-agent string, and the site that referred you. This is used to understand where visitors come from and to enforce limits on accounts that abuse the service.
Problem reports If you use "Report a problem", your message is stored together with the PDF you uploaded, so the failure can be reproduced.

How your documents are processed

This is the part most worth reading. To fill a worksheet, Paperfill sends the page (as text, as an image, or both) to third-party AI providers. Those providers receive the contents of your document. Currently these are:

  • The Hack Club AI proxy, which is the primary route for AI requests.
  • OpenRouter, used as a fallback when the primary route fails, which in turn routes requests to Google Gemini models.

Each of these providers handles your data under its own terms and privacy policy, and we cannot guarantee how long they retain requests or whether they use them to improve their own systems. If a document is confidential, do not upload it to Paperfill.

Other services we rely on

  • Supabase stores account records, usage counts, and subscription status.
  • Hack Club Nest hosts the application and its files.
  • Google provides sign-in when you choose it.
  • Whop processes Pro payments. Whop handles the transaction and we never see or store your card details; we receive only your email address and whether the subscription is active.
  • An email provider delivers account verification messages.
  • HilltopAds may serve video advertising to free accounts. When ads are enabled, the ad network receives your IP address and browser information and may set its own identifiers. Pro accounts are not shown ads.

How long we keep things

  • Uploaded documents and filled outputs: 30 days. They stay that long so you can reopen them from Past fills; after that they are deleted from the server automatically. You can delete any of them yourself, sooner, from Past fills.
  • Problem reports, including the attached PDF: 30 days.
  • Account information and usage counts: kept while your account exists.
  • Handwriting fonts: kept until you replace or delete them.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your documents to train our own models.
  • We do not read your documents except when you send a problem report, or where we have to investigate abuse of the service.

Children

Paperfill is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. You must be at least 13 to create an account. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us at paperfill@user.hackclub.app and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and everything attached to it by emailing paperfill@user.hackclub.app from the address on the account. You can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct it. You can clear the device cookie through your browser's settings at any time.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed, traffic is served over HTTPS, and uploaded files are removed on the schedule above. No service is perfectly secure, and Paperfill is a small independent project rather than a company with a security team. Please weigh that before uploading anything sensitive.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top of the page. Continuing to use Paperfill after a change means you accept the updated policy.
