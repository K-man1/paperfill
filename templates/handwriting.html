Set it up in three steps

Rebuild your handwriting

Print a template, write on it, upload it back. Takes about five minutes.

1. Print the template

Download the template (2 pages) and print it on plain paper.

Download template (PDF)

2. Fill it in, up to 3 times

Write each character in your own handwriting, staying inside the lines. For the most natural result, print and fill the template 2 or 3 times. We blend the copies so repeated letters don't look stamped. One copy works too.

3. Upload your copies

Upload each filled copy (both pages, as a PDF or photos). Copy 1 is required; 2 and 3 are optional but make your handwriting look more real.
